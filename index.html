About Us

Design is our
language.

At BENT Design Studio, we believe every home should respect how people live, age, and grow. With over a decade of experience in residential architecture and interiors, we bring together functionality, creativity, and Vaastu principles with practical site realities to create spaces that feel balanced, purposeful, and deeply personal.

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions. Our specialised philosophy focuses on:

Children's spaces designed for growth and play
Senior living designs that support comfort and independence

We see architecture and interiors as a continuous thread — not as separate disciplines. At Bent, we design spaces that are thoughtful in layout, respectful of context and culture, gentle to the environment, and resolved down to construction detail.

Our Design Philosophy

Designing For
Every Phase of Life

Life evolves. Homes should evolve with it. Different life stages bring different needs, and thoughtful design can make those transitions smoother, safer, and more meaningful.

Every decision is guided by one question: Will this improve the quality of life for the people using this space?

Our Full Story
The Problem
Most homes are designed for a single moment in time — not for the life that unfolds within them. Spaces become obstacles rather than enablers as families grow and change.
Our Thought Process
We ask questions that go beyond aesthetics — how will a child use this room in five years? How will a parent navigate this space as they age? What does comfort really look like here?
The Design Outcome
Spaces that feel natural. Homes that function effortlessly. Designs that remain relevant for years and environments that contribute positively to everyday living.
